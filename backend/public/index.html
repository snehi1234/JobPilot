What is HiresprintAI?

HiresprintAI is a free Chrome extension that helps job seekers — students and professionals alike — fill out online job application forms automatically. You fill in your details and upload your resume once; HiresprintAI then uses AI to read each job application form and generate suggested answers. AI can make mistakes; review all answers before submitting an application.

How it works

1. Sign in with Google and set up your profile (contact details, work history, resume) in the extension's side panel.
2. Open any supported job application page (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and more).
3. Click the floating "Autofill" button — HiresprintAI detects the form's fields and fills in suggested answers.
4. Alternatively, use the side panel to review and edit suggestions before applying them.
5. Automatic filling on page load is off by default. Enable it only after reading and agreeing to the disclosure in the panel. Always review the completed form, attach your resume, and submit it yourself.

Your privacy

Your saved details and resume are stored securely in your account. AI processing sends your profile, resume text, optional demographics, form fields, and job details to our AI service to generate answers. If you opt into automatic filling, this happens on recognized job pages without another click. Disable it or delete your saved profile from the panel.
