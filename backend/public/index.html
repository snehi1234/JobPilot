Questions

Is Hiresprint free?

Yes. Hiresprint is free to use, with a fair monthly usage limit so the service stays fast for everyone.

Which job sites does it work on?

Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, Taleo, SuccessFactors, and most company career sites. Match scores also show on LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor job pages. Some sites need you to sign in or click "Apply manually" first; Hiresprint tells you when.

Does it submit applications for me?

No. Hiresprint fills the form and shows a checklist of anything to review. You submit each application yourself.

Will the tailored resume make things up?

No. Tailoring only rewrites your own bullet points and never adds numbers, skills, or jobs. You approve every change.

Can I try it without applying anywhere?

Yes — after installing, open the practice application and click ⚡ Autofill. Nothing on it is ever submitted.

How do I get help?

Use "Report a problem" in the side panel, or email hiresprint.support@gmail.com.
