Software Engineer, Platform

Seattle, WA or Remote (US) · Full-time · Engineering

About the role

Northwind Labs builds logistics software used by thousands of warehouses. Our Platform team owns the services, APIs, and developer tooling every product team builds on.

What you'll do

  • Design and build reliable, scalable backend services and REST APIs in Python, Go, or Java.
  • Run services on Kubernetes and Docker in AWS or Azure, with CI/CD and infrastructure as code (Terraform).
  • Improve observability, on-call health, and performance of distributed systems.
  • Partner with product teams on API design, data models (PostgreSQL, Redis, Kafka), and security reviews.

What we're looking for

  • 3+ years building production software; strong computer science fundamentals.
  • Experience with microservices, cloud platforms, SQL, and automated testing.
  • Bonus: machine learning, LLM, or React/TypeScript experience.
