JobPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What we collect

JobPilot is a Chrome extension that autofills job application forms. To do this, we store the profile information you provide: your Quick Details (name, contact info, work authorization, optional voluntary demographics), any free-text notes you type, and the text extracted from a resume you upload. If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account identifier and email address to associate this data with your account. For autofill, we also process application form labels and choices, job posting details, and the site's hostname or job location. Monthly AI-request counters are stored per account.

How we use it

Your profile is used solely to generate and fill in answers on job application forms. When you request answers, the relevant profile and resume text, optional demographics, form fields, and job details are sent to our Vercel-hosted backend and third-party AI provider Groq. This release uses Groq's Free plan and does not fall back to another provider when its quota is exhausted. Automatic filling is off by default. If you explicitly enable it in the panel, this processing and filling can occur on recognized job application pages without another click or prior review. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored securely in our cloud database (Supabase), isolated per user account by authenticated backend requests. Direct client access to the database is blocked by row-level security. A copy is cached locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). Authentication tokens and extension preferences are stored locally. Automatic-filling consent is local to this browser and resets when you sign out or change accounts.

Your choices

You can view and edit your data at any time from the JobPilot side panel and turn automatic filling off to stop future automatic fills. The "Delete My Data" button removes your saved profile, resume text, and demographics from the profile database and this browser, and disables automatic filling. Signing out clears the local profile cache but does not delete your cloud profile.

Data retention

Saved profile data is retained until you delete it. Monthly usage counters are retained separately to enforce the usage limit and are not removed by the profile-deletion button. Contact us about other retained records. Processing and retention by hosting and AI providers are also subject to their applicable service terms and privacy policies.

Chrome Web Store data use

JobPilot's use and transfer of user data adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide and maintain the disclosed job-application autofill functionality. We do not sell user data, transfer it outside approved use cases, use it for unrelated purposes, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Children's privacy

JobPilot is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
