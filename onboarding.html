Add your details & resume

Open the JobPilot side panel, fill in your Quick Details, and upload your resume (PDF or Word). Your details are saved securely to your JobPilot account.

3

Choose how to fill applications

On a job form (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and more), click the floating ✨ Autofill button to fill directly, or use the panel to review suggestions before applying them. Automatic filling on page load is off by default; enable it only if you agree to the disclosure in the panel. Review the completed form, attach your resume manually, and submit.

AI processing sends your profile, resume text, optional demographics, form fields, and job details to JobPilot's AI service. With automatic filling enabled, this happens when recognized job pages load. AI can make mistakes. Delete your saved profile or disable automatic filling from the panel.
