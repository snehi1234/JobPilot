Add your details & resumes

Open the Hiresprint side panel, fill in your Quick Details, and upload up to five resumes (PDF or Word) — for example one for software roles and one for data roles. Your details are saved securely to your Hiresprint account.

3

Fill applications in seconds

On a job form (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and more), click the floating ⚡ Autofill button. Hiresprint picks the resume that best matches the job, attaches it, adds a cover letter if you want one, and shows a checklist of anything to review. Automatic filling on page load is off by default; enable it only if you agree to the disclosure in the panel. Review the completed form, then submit it yourself.

AI processing sends your profile, resume text, optional demographics, form fields, and job details to Hiresprint's AI service. With automatic filling enabled, this happens when recognized job pages load. AI can make mistakes. Delete your saved profile or disable automatic filling from the panel.
