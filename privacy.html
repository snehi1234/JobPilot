Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

What we collect

Hiresprint stores the profile information you provide — your Quick Details (name, contact, work authorization, optional demographics), any notes you type, and the text extracted from the resumes you upload (up to five). If you sign in with Google, we store your Google account identifier and email to associate this data with your account. For autofill, we also process application form labels and choices, job posting details, and the site's hostname or job location. Monthly AI-request counters are stored per account.

How we use it

Your profile is used solely to fill out job application forms on your behalf. When you request answers, the relevant profile and resume text, optional demographics, form fields, and job details are sent to our servers and processed by a third-party AI service provider that generates the answers on our behalf. Automatic filling is off by default. If you explicitly enable it in the panel, this processing and filling can occur on recognized job application pages without another click or prior review. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data is stored securely with our cloud database provider, isolated per user account, and cached locally in your browser for performance. Access is protected by your Google sign-in. Authentication tokens and extension preferences are stored locally. The original resume files you upload stay only in this browser, so Hiresprint can attach the matching file to applications; only their extracted text is synced. A cover letter file you upload is also kept only in this browser. If you choose AI-written cover letters, the resume selected for a job and the job details are sent to our AI service to write the letter, which is saved in this browser so it can be reused and edited. Corrections you make to filled answers and answers you save for reuse are also kept only in this browser. Answers to common, non-job-specific questions are remembered only in your browser so repeat applications need fewer AI requests; they are cleared whenever your profile changes, you sign out, or you delete your data. Automatic-filling consent is local to this browser and resets when you sign out or change accounts.

Your choices

You can view and edit your data anytime in the Hiresprint panel. You can turn automatic filling off at any time to stop future automatic fills. The "Delete My Data" button removes your saved profile, resumes, saved answers, and demographics from the profile database and this browser, and disables automatic filling. Signing out clears the local profile cache but does not delete your cloud profile. Monthly usage counters are retained separately to enforce the usage limit.
